# Community guidelines

Treat participants with respect and keep discussion focused on reproducible evidence. Critique methods, measurements, code, and claims with enough context for another person to verify the point.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, deliberate disclosure of private information, and manipulation of benchmark evidence are unacceptable. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when these guidelines are violated.

Report sensitive conduct concerns through the repository's private maintainer contact after publication. Public issues are suitable for process questions that contain no personal or confidential information.
